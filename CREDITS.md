# Credits and third-party materials

This file credits people and community projects that helped make **X-Com: From the Ashes** possible.

From the Ashes builds on the work of the OpenXcom community. Some materials are original FTA work, some were contributed directly by community members, and some are adapted from OpenXcom mods or community resources with author permission and/or under compatible community licensing terms.

Special thanks and respect to the engine projects that made FTA possible:
